RV328 analog macro specification

The digital core talks to one analog block, rv328_analog, through the ports below. Two views exist today: rtl/analog/rv328_analog_model.v (behavioural, used in simulation - treat it as the executable version of this specification) and rtl/analog/rv328_analog_stub.v (synthesisable placeholder for the digital-first tapeout).

Status. A transistor-level design of every block now exists in analog/, simulated against the foundry models over corners, supply and temperature, and assembled into one netlist with these ports (analog/blocks/rv328_analog.spice). analog/README.md has the results against the targets below and the list of deviations. Every block has a layout, and the blocks are assembled into one macro layout with these ports (analog/layout/: 1380 x 927 um, DRC and LVS clean, simulated from its extraction). It has no supply or guard rings, its pins are where its wires end (44 at the foot of a trunk on one edge, the ten analog inputs on the same edge, the crystal pins on the opposite one), and there is no LEF or liberty file, so the chip layout still uses the stub. At 1.28 mm2 there is barely room for it beside the digital layout in the half-height slot: a trial layout with its room reserved (cells at 74 % of the rows left, against 46 % today) routes, but has no room for the flow's repair steps, and one register's fan-out misses 20 MHz at the slow corner (docs/layout_results.md, "Trial with the analog block"). The targets it does not meet as designed: the 1.8 V brown-out level (2.3 V instead), the accuracy of the 128 kHz oscillator without trim, and a rail-to-rail comparator input. The ADC front end and the power-down current, which were wrong after the first design round, were corrected in the second. A third round followed the first layouts: the ADC comparator, which took most of a bit time to reverse a decision, was made ten times faster, and every large resistor was moved to the 1 kohm-per-square film, the only high-resistance film this process variant has. The ADC's code transitions are within half an LSB in simulation.

Supply: AVCC pad (analog), VSS. All digital ports are 5 V-domain CMOS levels (the core runs from the pad supply).

Integration notes

  • Every output of the macro crosses into the digital core through a synchroniser except the clocks and por_n.
  • clk_rc, clk_xa and clk_lf feed a glitch-free clock multiplexer; each must stop and start without partial pulses when its enable changes.
  • In power-down the core drives rc_en and xo_en low and raises them again asynchronously on a wake event.
  • The pads carrying analog signals are plain gf180mcu_fd_io__asig_5p0 cells; the digital function of the same package pin uses a separate bi_24t pad and a second bond wire, so no custom pad cell is needed.
  • For layout, the macro needs a LEF with the ports above, a liberty file for the digital ports (clock outputs declared as clocks) and GDS; add it to flow/librelane/macros.yaml, remove the stub from config.yaml and replace the stub clocks in chip_top.sdc.
